# Memory

_Curated long-term knowledge. Empty at birth — grows through sessions._

_Four registers, nothing else. Compact lines, linked by identifier (P = problem, H = hypothesis, E = experiment). No personal data about colleagues: people appear by role only. Aim to stay under roughly 1500 tokens, a guardrail rather than a hard gate; when it is exceeded, compress the closed items (`validated` or `invalidated` hypotheses, finished experiments) to one line each. Raw session notes go in `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. See `references/memory-guidance.md`._

_Register headings and status values: if this file already has some, keep them as they are, in whatever language; new ones are written in English, as below (see `references/memory-guidance.md`)._

## Hospital-software teams and products
_Hospital-software teams and products. Format: `team or product — one-line context — last session`_

## Framed problems
_Framed problems. Format: `P<n> | team or product | one-sentence statement | users concerned | remaining unknowns | date`_

## Hypotheses
_Hypotheses. Format: `H<n> | P<n> | statement | risk (value, usability, feasibility, viability) | status: to test, validated or invalidated | evidence | date`_

## Experiments
_Experiments. Format: `E<n> | H<n> | format | decision threshold set beforehand | result (or in progress) | date`_
