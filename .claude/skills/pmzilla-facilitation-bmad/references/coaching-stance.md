# Work on a coaching stance

## What Success Looks Like

The user gets a reading of the stance expected in a given situation, with what it takes to hold it. The deliverable contains:

- the expected stance (coach, Scrum Master, PO), stated by what it does and does not do in this situation;
- the tricky situations, those where the stance slips (giving the answer, taking the group's place, arbitrating without having said so);
- opening phrases, short, that the person who accompanies can take up in their own words.
