# Memory

_Curated long-term knowledge. Empty at birth — grows through sessions._

_Four registers, nothing else. Compact lines, linked by identifier (T = team or topology change, D = dependency, O = organisation decision, K = OKR project, P = platform). No personal data about colleagues: people appear by role only. Aim to stay under roughly 1500 tokens, a guardrail rather than a hard gate; when it is exceeded, compress the closed items (removed dependencies, decisions whose effect is observed, finished projects) to one line each, and fold old topology states into the current one with a dated note of what changed. Raw session notes go in `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. See `references/memory-guidance.md`._

_Register headings and status values: if this file already has some, keep them as they are, in whatever language; new ones are written in English, as below (see `references/memory-guidance.md`)._

## Team topology
_Team topology. Team format: `T<n> | team | type (stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-subsystem, undetermined) | value stream served | cognitive load points | date`_
_Change format: `T<n> | topology change | before → after | reason | date`_

## Dependencies between teams
_Dependencies between teams. Format: `D<n> | waiting team | awaited team | nature | coordination mechanism in place | status: open, being handled or removed | date`_

## Organisation decisions
_Organisation decisions. Format: `O<n> | decision in one sentence | D<n> or T<n> concerned | expected effect | observed effect (or not yet observed) | date`_

## OKR projects and platforms
_OKR projects and platforms. OKR format: `K<n> | scope | prerequisites (product maturity, team stability): established, partial or missing | state | date`_
_Platform format: `P<n> | platform | internal users | adoption measure | state | date`_
